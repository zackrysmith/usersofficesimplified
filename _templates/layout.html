Download, install, or reinstall Microsoft 365 or Office 2024 on a PC or Mac Already Purchased

If you have already purchased Microsoft 365 or a version of Microsoft Office, you do not normally need to buy it again when setting up a new computer. You can use the Microsoft account associated with your purchase to download, install, activate, or reinstall the software on a compatible PC or Mac.

This guide explains how to install Office 365 already purchased, reinstall Microsoft 365, use an Office product key, and activate Office after installing it on another device. It also covers common searches such as Microsoft 365.com setup, Microsoft 365 setup product key, and office.com/setup home & student 2024.

I just purchased Microsoft 365, how do I install it?

If you have just purchased Microsoft 365, the first step is to sign in with the Microsoft account used during the purchase. Your subscription or license should be associated with that account.

  1. Open your web browser and go to the official Microsoft account or Microsoft 365 website.
  2. Select the option to sign in.
  3. Complete the Microsoft account sign in using the account connected to your purchase.
  4. Look for your Microsoft 365 subscription or Office product.
  5. Select the option to install Microsoft 365 or Office.
  6. Download the installation file for your PC or Mac.
  7. Run the installer and follow the instructions on screen.
  8. Open Word, Excel, or another Office application after installation.
  9. Sign in with the same Microsoft account to complete activation.

This is generally the easiest way to install Microsoft 365 already purchased because your license information is connected to your Microsoft account.

How to install Office 365 already purchased

Many people search for install office 365 already purchased when they have bought Microsoft 365 previously but are setting it up on a new computer. The installation process depends on whether you purchased a Microsoft 365 subscription or a one-time Office license.

For a Microsoft 365 subscription, sign in to your Microsoft account and locate your subscription. From there, choose the installation option and download the appropriate installer.

Once the installer has downloaded, open it and allow the setup process to complete. Afterward, launch an Office application and sign in when prompted.

Microsoft 365.com setup and account sign-in

The term Microsoft 365.com setup is commonly used to describe the process of accessing your Microsoft 365 account, downloading Office, and completing installation. You may also see searches such as www.office365.com/setup or microsoft365.com/setup/personal.

When using a Microsoft website for setup, make sure that you are using the official Microsoft website rather than an unrelated third-party download page. Avoid entering your Microsoft account password or product key into websites that are not operated by Microsoft.

Microsoft 365 login my account

Your Microsoft account is important because Microsoft can associate your subscription or purchased Office license with that account. If you already have an account, use the same credentials you used when purchasing or redeeming Office.

For Microsoft 365 login my account, open the Microsoft sign-in page and enter your email address, phone number, or Skype name associated with the account. Enter your password and complete any security verification requested.

You may also see the term microsoft 365 sign in account when searching for the login page. The important thing is to use the Microsoft account connected to your Office purchase.

Where to enter your Office product key

If you purchased a retail copy of Office that includes a product key, you may need to redeem the key before installing the software. A product key is usually a 25-character code made up of letters and numbers.

If you are wondering where to enter your Office product key, use Microsoft's official Office setup and redemption process. Sign in with your Microsoft account when requested and enter the product key in the appropriate field.

After the key has been successfully redeemed, the Office license can become associated with your Microsoft account. In many cases, future installations can then be handled through the account without repeatedly entering the same key.

Microsoft office product key and Microsoft 365 setup product key

A Microsoft office product key can be used to redeem certain Office purchases. However, not every Microsoft 365 installation requires you to enter a product key during installation.

If you purchased Microsoft 365 directly through Microsoft or another authorized retailer and the subscription is already linked to your Microsoft account, signing in may be enough to activate the product.

The phrase Microsoft 365 setup product key can therefore refer to the product-key redemption stage of setup. If your purchase included a key, keep it private and enter it only through Microsoft's official redemption process.

Setting up Microsoft 365 with a product key

Setting up Microsoft 365 with a product key usually involves two separate steps: redeeming the key and installing the software.

  1. Locate the product key included with your purchase.
  2. Go to Microsoft's official Office product-key redemption page.
  3. Sign in with your Microsoft account.
  4. Enter the product key when prompted.
  5. Follow the instructions to associate the license with your account.
  6. Return to your Microsoft account and locate the Office product.
  7. Choose the installation option.
  8. Install Office on your PC or Mac.
  9. Open an Office application and sign in if prompted.

Enter product key Microsoft Office: What if I already have an account?

Reinstall Microsoft 365 already purchased

If Microsoft 365 was previously installed on your computer but you removed it, replaced your hard drive, reset the operating system, or purchased a new computer, you may be able to install it again without purchasing another subscription.

To Reinstall Microsoft 365 already purchased, sign in to the Microsoft account associated with your subscription. Find your Microsoft 365 product and select the installation option.

After downloading the installer, run it and allow the installation to finish. Open Word, Excel, or another Office application and sign in with the same account if activation is requested.

Activate Office after reinstalling or installing on a different device

If you have installed Office on a new computer, you may need to sign in before the applications become fully activated. This is especially common with Microsoft 365 subscriptions because the subscription is managed through your Microsoft account.

For Activate Office after reinstalling or installing on a different device, open an Office application such as Word. When prompted, sign in using the account associated with your Office license.

If your subscription permits installation on multiple devices, you can use the account to manage your available installations. If you have reached a device limit, you may need to remove an old device from your account before activating the new one.

How do I activate my Microsoft Office subscription?

If you are asking, How do i activate my Microsoft office subscription, the answer usually starts with your Microsoft account. After installing Microsoft 365, open an Office application and sign in using the account that owns the subscription.

If the application displays an activation prompt, follow the instructions shown on screen. Check that you are using the correct account and that your Microsoft 365 subscription is active.

Microsoft 365 product key activation

Microsoft 365 product key activation can be slightly different depending on where you purchased the subscription. A retail purchase may provide a product key that needs to be redeemed, while a subscription purchased directly through Microsoft may already be connected to your account.

If you have a product key, redeem it through the official Microsoft process. Once the license is associated with your account, sign in during Office installation or activation.

Can I activate Microsoft 365 with a product key?

Yes, certain Microsoft 365 purchases can involve a product key. If your purchase supplied a key, you can redeem it through Microsoft's official redemption process.

After redemption, your Microsoft account becomes particularly important because it can be used to access and manage the product. Therefore, if you want to activate Microsoft 365 with product key, make sure you redeem the key using the correct Microsoft account.

Microsoft Word product key

Some users search specifically for a Microsoft world product key, usually meaning a Microsoft Word product key. Word is part of several Microsoft Office and Microsoft 365 editions, so activation generally depends on the Office license rather than a separate Word-only key.

If Word asks you to activate, sign in with the Microsoft account associated with your Office purchase. If you purchased a retail product with a key, make sure the key has been properly redeemed.

PC and Mac installation differences

Microsoft 365 and compatible Office editions are available for both Windows PCs and Macs, but the installation files and system requirements can differ.

For Windows, download the installer provided for your Office license and follow the Windows installation prompts. On a Mac, download the Mac-compatible Office installer and follow the macOS installation instructions.

Before installing, check Microsoft's current system requirements to make sure your operating system and hardware are supported by the Office version you purchased.

What if Office says my product is not activated?

If Office displays an activation warning, start with a few basic checks:

  • Make sure you are connected to the internet.
  • Confirm that you signed in with the correct Microsoft account.
  • Check whether your Microsoft 365 subscription is still active.
  • Confirm that the Office edition installed matches your purchase.
  • If you purchased a product key, verify that it was successfully redeemed.
  • Restart the Office application after signing in.
  • Install available Office updates.

If the problem continues, Microsoft's official support resources can help identify account, subscription, installation, or activation issues.

Common mistakes to avoid during Microsoft 365 setup

Installing Office should be straightforward, but a few common mistakes can cause unnecessary problems.

Using a different Microsoft account

If the license belongs to one Microsoft account but you sign in with another account, Office may appear to be unavailable or unlicensed. Always try the account used to purchase or redeem the product.

Downloading Office from unofficial websites

Search results can contain websites offering modified Office installers or suspicious product keys. Avoid these downloads. Use Microsoft's official website for account access, downloads, product-key redemption, and support.

Confusing Microsoft 365 with Office 2024

Microsoft 365 is generally subscription-based, while Office 2024 is a one-time purchase edition. Their features, licensing, and installation options can differ. Check your purchase confirmation before choosing an installer.

Sharing your product key

Your product key should remain private. Do not publish it online or give it to an unknown person offering to activate Office for you.
